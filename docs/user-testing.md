# User testing: facilitator script

The testing kit is at `/ar/test` (Arabic) and `/en/test` (English). It is not indexed, saves nothing and sends nothing. Testers copy their answers and send them to you.

## Who to invite

- 5 graduates or final-year students (the participant tasks).
- 3 people who hire or manage interns at a company (the company tasks).

Five people per group find most of the big problems. Mix fields and include at least two people who prefer Arabic.

## Before the session (5 minutes)

1. Open the kit in the tester's language on their own phone or laptop.
2. Say: "We are testing the website, not you. There are no wrong answers. Nothing you type is saved."
3. Ask them to think out loud: what they expect, what they look for, what confuses them.

## During each task

- Read the task once. Do not explain where to click.
- If they are stuck for more than a minute, ask "What would you try next?" before helping.
- Note: where they hesitated, what they clicked first, words they did not understand, anything they said out loud.
- After the task, they choose how easy it was and write where they got stuck.

## Tasks

| Who | Task | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participant | Find a project in your field and try to join it | `/projects` |
| Participant | Book a seat in the CV workshop | `/workshops/cv-thirty-seconds/book` |
| Participant | Tick a finished step, then hand in your work | `/me/projects/cafe-launch-campaign` |
| Participant | Use the CV line checker | `/guides/cv` |
| Company | Post a new project | `/org/new-project` |
| Company | Review a participant's work and ask for changes | `/org/projects/cafe-launch-campaign/review/p3` |
| Company | Choose participants for an apply-first project | `/org/projects/loyalty-programme` |

## At the end (5 minutes)

- The kit asks: would they use Forsa today, and the one thing they would change.
- They press "Copy your answers" and paste them into a message to you.

## After all sessions

1. Put every answer in one sheet: tester, task, ease (1 to 5), where they got stuck, your notes.
2. Any task with an average ease below 3, or where 2 or more people got stuck in the same place, is a problem to fix first.
3. Write the top five problems with one example quote each, and share them before the next build slice.

Keep testers' names out of the sheet; use "P1", "C1" and so on.
